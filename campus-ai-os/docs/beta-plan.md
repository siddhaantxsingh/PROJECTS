# Beta plan (not started — requires the owner)

Goal: learn whether students use grounded Q&A and study tools on real course material, with no fabricated metrics.

1. Recruit 1–2 instructors willing to upload their own lecture notes (with permission to process them) for one course each.
2. Deploy on a host the owner controls, behind HTTPS, with backups (`docs/deployment.md`). Turn on `CAMPUS_SECURE_COOKIES=1`.
3. Measure only what the system records: questions per student per week, abstention rate, thumbs-up share, quiz attempts. Add a short opt-in survey at week 4.
4. Success signals to decide on: abstention rate below 30 % on real questions; at least 40 % of active students returning in week 3; instructor-reported usefulness of the topic analytics.
5. Before any launch: privacy notice explaining what is stored (questions are visible to no one individually; deleted with the account), data-retention period, and a contact for deletion requests.
