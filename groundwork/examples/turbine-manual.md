# Wind Turbine Maintenance Manual (example document, fictional)

## Gearbox

The gearbox must be lubricated every 500 operating hours. Use ISO VG 320 gear oil.
Check the oil level weekly while the rotor is locked.

## Blades

Inspect the blades once a year. Cracks longer than 5 mm require blade replacement.
Leading-edge erosion is repaired with polyurethane tape.

## Safety

Lock out the rotor before climbing the tower. Two technicians must be present at all times.
